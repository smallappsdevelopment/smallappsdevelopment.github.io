Privacy Policy

Effective 22 November 2026

This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the following apps by Small Apps Dev ("we", "us"):

  • Notch Remover and Notch Remover Pro
  • Notches | Notch for Android and Notches Pro
  • Rounded: Corners Curve and its Pro version

In short

  • We don't ask for, collect or store any personal information. There are no accounts.
  • Your settings stay on your device.
  • The free apps show ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some device data to show and measure those ads, as described below.
  • The Pro apps show no ads and don't include the ads SDK.
  • We don't sell your data.

What the apps do on your device

The apps draw decorations over the screen edges: they hide or add a notch, round the corners and style the navigation bar. To do this they use the "Display over other apps" permission. The apps only draw over the screen; they don't read, record or capture what is shown on it.

Your choices, such as the selected decoration, its size and opacity, are saved on your device only. They are removed when you uninstall the app.

Advertising in the free apps

The free versions of the apps show ads using the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob), provided by Google LLC. When an ad is requested or shown, the SDK may collect and send to Google:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate your approximate location;
  • interactions with ads, such as views and taps;
  • diagnostic and performance information, such as app launch time and crash information related to ads.

Google uses this information to show ads, measure them, limit how often you see them and prevent fraud, and, where you have agreed to it, to show ads based on your interests. Data is encrypted in transit. Google handles this data under its own privacy policy:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Data disclosed by the Google Mobile Ads SDK

We receive only aggregated reports from AdMob, such as the number of ads shown. These reports don't identify you.

Your choices

Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the free apps ask for your consent before ads are personalized, using Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time from Privacy options in the app's More tab. If you don't consent, you may still see non-personalized or limited ads.

US state privacy laws

Where US state laws give you the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for targeted advertising, you can opt out through the same Privacy options entry where it is shown.

Advertising ID

You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your phone's settings, under Settings → Google → All services → Ads (the exact path depends on the phone).

Removing ads

The Pro version of each app has no ads and no ads SDK.

Permissions

  • Display over other apps: to draw the decorations.
  • Foreground service: to keep the decorations on screen while you use other apps.
  • Run at startup: to restore the decorations after the phone restarts.
  • Internet and network state: to load ads in the free apps.
  • Advertising ID: used by the ads SDK in the free apps, as described above.

Children

The apps are not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has used a free app and you are concerned about the data described above, contact us and we will help.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data. Because we don't collect or store personal data ourselves, we have nothing to return or delete. Data collected for ads is held by Google; you can manage it through your consent choices above and through My Ad Center. You can also contact us with any question about your data, and you have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when an app adds a feature. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
